- **Correction scope.** A collapsed lab requires two taps to correct an individual period. Leave-linked or excluded-period corrections still go through the sheet. The verification pass leaves those paths in place.
- **Visible action labels.** Compact Present and Absent controls use accessible names and glyphs. The glyphs carry the action on screen when the control switches to compact mode.
- **Large text.** Check 1.0×, 1.3× and 2.0× on a physical phone, including a narrow window and increased Android display size. Check tab text and touch targets, Day row wrapping, Calendar cells, long names, and scrolling or keyboard overlap in forms and sheets. The 393dp host captures cover a subset of these cases.
- **TalkBack.** Verify focus order and announcements on the bottom bar and rail, Present/Absent selection and custom actions, collapsed and expanded labs, the completion live region, Calendar status markers, sheet controls, and focus return after dialogs. Check whether the period sheet's **Counts toward attendance** text is announced with its switch.
- **Wide navigation.** Check the labelled rail at and above 600dp and the bottom bar below it using a tablet, foldable or resized window. Check rotation, labels at 2.0×, content width and tab/back-stack behavior across the breakpoint.
- **Motion and platform UI.** Check tab and day transitions, the selected indicator, lab expansion, snackbars and the date/time pickers at normal and disabled system animation scales. Exercise Back gestures, reminder notification actions, and the Android backup file picker on an isolated fixture.
